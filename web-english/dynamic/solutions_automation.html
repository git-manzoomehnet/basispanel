Manage Organizational Communications with the Secretariat Module

Designed as a hub for organizational communications, the Secretariat Module provides the ability to manage and monitor incoming and outgoing messages and tickets. With this module, organizations can optimize their communication processes and engage in structured internal interactions.

Advanced Ticketing System
Streamlined Ticket Management & Simplified Internal Communications
Improved Team Coordination & Effective Message Tracking
Complete Oversight of Tickets and Effective Communication

This module provides the ability to view and manage all sent and received tickets. Additionally, you can send tickets simultaneously to other team members by using features like BCC and CC.

Structured Management for Accurate Monitoring and Follow-up

With the help of the Secretariat Module, messages and tickets can be precisely and centrally tracked. As a result, managers and users can efficiently handle all interactions without losing key information, and the process of responding to and following up on messages is also expedited.
