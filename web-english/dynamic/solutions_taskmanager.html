Ability to Use Scrum and Kanban

With support for Scrum and Kanban methodologies, Basis Panel provides high flexibility in agile (Agile) project management. Teams can adopt the Scrum approach to benefit from structured, step-by-step sprints. Moreover, using Kanban allows for visual process management, making it easier to track and prioritize tasks. This flexibility helps teams choose the methodology that best meets their project needs, leading to more optimized operations and greater transparency.

Epic Management

The ability to manage epics allows teams to break down large, complex projects into smaller, more manageable parts within the Basis Panel environment. This structure helps maintain a clear overview of project progress and ensures alignment with business goals. Moreover, stakeholders can track the project's progress and understand how each task contributes to overarching objectives.

Sprint Planning

Teams can use this module's sprint planning feature to estimate tasks, allocate resources, and set realistic timelines. This feature supports a regular delivery cycle, prevents overcommitment, and keeps the team aligned with goals. By planning sprints effectively in Basis Panel, teams can maintain a balanced, stable workflow while satisfying stakeholders with the team's progress.

Issue Tracking

Because it offers centralized issue tracking, the project management module serves as a reliable resource for monitoring problems, team tasks, and other requirements. With this tracking system, teams can respond quickly to issues, set priorities, and follow up on problem resolution. This feature enhances product quality and provides better control over project delivery.
