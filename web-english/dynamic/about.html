Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) platforms are the backbone of decision-making and management for modern businesses. These centralized platforms seamlessly unify all information and data related to finance, human resources, supply, production, customers, and many other elements into a single shared location.

The Origin Story of Basis Panel

Basis Panel was developed by Manzoomeh Negaran with the goal of providing an integrated, accessible platform for managing personal, corporate, and e-commerce issues. This product is the result of more than 26 years of experience and effort by the Manzoomeh Negaran team, which has provided web-based services to over a thousand Iranian companies. Basis Panel meets diverse user needs by combining features from three different systems in one place. At the personal level, it offers features such as calendar, notes, file sharing, and email checking. At the corporate level, it includes subsystems for data management, project execution, and ticket sales tools for resource planning. At the e-commerce level, it offers website management, data tracking, CDN cluster management, and SEO optimization. With a design similar to desktop applications, Basis Panel ensures ease of use and is recognized as a powerful tool for unified organizational management. By offering extensive and efficient features, it has played an important role in improving processes and increasing productivity for many companies.
